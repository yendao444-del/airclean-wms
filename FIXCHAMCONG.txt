1. Van de da xac dinh

- Loi UnicodeEncodeError trong attendance_service.py
  Nguyen nhan:
  Python print emoji/Unicode ra console Windows dung cp1252, lam service crash ngay khi startup.
  Trang thai:
  Da fix bang 2 lop bao ve:
  - attendance_service.py ep stdout/stderr sang utf-8
  - Electron spawn them PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8

- Loi health check yeu tren port 5001
  Nguyen nhan:
  App truoc day chi can port 5001 co response la coi nhu face service san sang.
  Rui ro:
  Nhan nham process khac dang nghe cung port.
  Trang thai:
  Da fix:
  - /status phai tra ve service=status=version dung contract
  - Electron validate payload, khong chi check HTTP 200

- Loi port conflict 5001
  Nguyen nhan:
  Co process cu/khac dang giu port 5001, app spawn service moi qua som nen bi Errno 10048.
  Trang thai:
  Da fix:
  - sua logic kill port
  - doi va verify port 5001 free truoc khi spawn

- Loi timeout startup tren may khach
  Nguyen nhan:
  attendance_service.py dang load_encodings() trong startup.
  Neu encodings.pkl lech voi thu muc faces thi service rebuild toan bo truoc khi mo port 5001.
  May co nhieu anh se rebuild rat lau, app timeout truoc khi service ready.
  Trang thai:
  [DONE] Đã fix bằng cách:
  - Tách load_encodings() sang background thread.
  - Service lấy port 5001 ngay lập tức.

- May khach dang chay bang Python fallback thay vi attendance_service.exe
  Nguyen nhan:
  Goi phat hanh hien tai chua coi EXE la duong production bat buoc.

  Trang thai:
  [DONE] Đã fix: Bổ sung bước pyinstaller build EXE thẳng vào RELEASE.bat và RELEASE-ver2.bat.
